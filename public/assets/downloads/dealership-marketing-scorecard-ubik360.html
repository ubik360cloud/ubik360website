How To Use This

Score yourself honestly on each question.

For each question, mark whether the answer is genuinely yes. Most dealerships answer "no" or "not sure" to at least half of these — that's normal, and it's exactly what this scorecard is built to surface. Nothing here requires guessing; if you're not sure, that's a "no."

1

Does every vehicle in your physical inventory appear in your Google and Meta ads automatically?

If a unit sells and the ad doesn't pull down same-day, you're paying to advertise cars you can't sell.

Yes / No
2

Do you know your average lead response time, in minutes, right now?

Dealers who respond within 5 minutes are roughly 10x more likely to make contact than those who wait 30.

Yes / No
3

Can you say exactly what your cost-per-lead was last month, by channel?

Not total spend — cost per lead, broken out by Google, Meta, and any other channel you run.

Yes / No
4

Is your ad budget reallocated toward what's working in real time, or reviewed on a fixed schedule?

A monthly review means weeks of underperforming spend before anything changes.

Yes / No
5

If you manage multiple rooftops, can you push a message or promotion across all of them in minutes?

Not applicable if single-location — otherwise, this is a common bottleneck during time-sensitive sales events.

Yes / No
6

Do you have a live dashboard for ad performance, or do you wait for a monthly report?

If you can't check performance today, you're always making decisions on stale data.

Yes / No
7

Can you trace a sold vehicle back to the specific ad or campaign that generated the lead?

Without real attribution, you're guessing which channels actually sell cars versus just generate clicks.

Yes / No
8

Are your Google Business Profile and reviews actively managed, not just monitored?

97% of shoppers check a business's online presence before visiting — an unmanaged profile is a silent leak.

Yes / No
9

Do your promotions run across a full funnel — landing page, banners, retargeting, email — or just one channel?

A single-channel promo reaches far fewer in-market shoppers than a coordinated push.

Yes / No
10

If your marketing stopped producing results tomorrow, would you know why within a day?

This is really a question about visibility — not performance. You can't fix what you can't see.

Yes / No
Scoring: 8–10 "yes" answers — your systems are ahead of most dealerships; the gains left are marginal. 4–7 — there's real, recoverable revenue being left on the table. 0–3 — your marketing and your inventory aren't talking to each other yet, and that's usually the single biggest fix available.
