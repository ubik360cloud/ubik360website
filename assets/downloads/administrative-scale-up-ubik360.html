The problem isn't the people—it's the model. You're using a system designed for an era when all work had to happen in the same building. Back-office work—bookkeeping, scheduling, data entry, customer follow-up, appointment setting—doesn't require geographic proximity. It requires reliability, communication, and skill. All of which exist in abundance in Colombia.

  • Hire locally at U.S./Canada market rates
  • Pay 25–35% on top for employer taxes and benefits

  • Repeat the entire cycle

The New Model

  • Hire bilingual Colombian professionals at nearshore rates
  • Managed onboarding, supervision, and accountability included

  • Scale up or down without HR overhead

Most businesses spend more time deliberating about nearshore staffing than it actually takes to implement it. Here's how the process works from the moment you say yes.

1

We map out exactly what you need: tasks, tools, hours, communication cadence, and success metrics. This 60-minute conversation prevents 90% of the problems that come from vague job descriptions and misaligned expectations.

2

Candidate Sourcing & Vetting (Days 4–12)

I source from my existing network and vetted talent pool. Every candidate is screened for English proficiency, relevant experience, and cultural fit with your business type. You receive 2–3 finalist profiles with my recommendation on who to move forward with.

3

Your Interview (Days 13–15)

You meet your top candidate on a video call. I'm present to facilitate and translate context where needed. You make the final call. If it's not the right fit, we move to the next candidate—no delay, no restart.

4

Onboarding (Days 16–25)

I handle the contract, tool access, and workflow orientation. Your new team member starts contributing within the first week—not the first month. I run daily check-ins for the first two weeks to ensure everything is on track.

5

First Deliverable (Day 30)

By the end of the first month, your team member is fully operational. You've already received measurable output—processed invoices, booked appointments, organized inbox, completed reports—whatever your role requires. The ROI clock starts on Day 1, not Day 90.

No long-term lock-in to start. We begin with a 90-day engagement. If the arrangement isn't delivering results after 90 days, we adjust the role, the person, or the structure. You're not signing a multi-year staffing contract.
