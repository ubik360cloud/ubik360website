Implementation

How to Launch
in 30 Days

You don't need a six-month runway to start capturing Hispanic market leads. Here's a practical 30-day roadmap to go from zero to a working lead engine.

1

Week 1 — Audit & Setup

Set up Google Local Services Ads (LSA) if eligible for your service category (roofing, HVAC, remodeling, plumbing). LSA is particularly effective because it shows verified badges that build trust immediately. Simultaneously, create a Meta Business Manager account structured for geographic targeting within your service radius.

2

Week 2 — Native Content Creation

Write ad copy, landing page copy, and follow-up sequences in native Spanish. This is not a translation step—it's a creation step. The tone, the pain points, the social proof format, and the CTAs need to be written for this audience from scratch. Avoid any copy that reads like it was run through a translation tool.

3

Week 3 — Geo-Targeted Campaign Launch

Launch Meta Ads with a 5–10 mile geo-radius targeting Spanish-language audiences. Start with a $500–$1,000 test budget. Run parallel creative variants—at least two ad formats—to identify what resonates fastest. Google LSA runs on a pay-per-lead model, so activate it alongside Meta for full-funnel coverage.

4

Week 4 — Nearshore Bridge Activation

Bring your Colombian appointment setter team online. These are bilingual professionals who handle incoming leads in Spanish, qualify them against your criteria, and book directly into your calendar. Onboarding typically takes 3–5 days. By end of Week 4, your first Spanish-sourced leads should be showing up as booked appointments in English.

5

Ongoing — Optimize & Scale

Review lead quality weekly for the first 60 days. Refine ad targeting based on which zip codes, demographics, and creative perform best. Scale budget on winning combinations. The goal by Day 90: a self-sustaining lead engine that delivers qualified, pre-vetted appointments without you touching the Spanish side of the funnel at all.
